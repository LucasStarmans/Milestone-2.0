Why?

We founded and created this site to enable users such as road users, and traffic analysts to gain an easier and more efficient understanding of road issues throughout Victoria. From our own observations of heightened road incidents, we decided to take action. This site transforms raw data into real and clear visual stories. We support and empower informed decisions that lead to safer roads and stronger communities, all thanks to data-driven insights.
