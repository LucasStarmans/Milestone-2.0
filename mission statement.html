Our Mission

Built to empower Victorians with transparent, data-driven insights into road incidents to help reduce crashes and save lives. We aim to make complex transport data accessible and meaningful for citizens, planners, and policymakers. By turning raw statistics into clear visual stories, we support informed decisions that lead to safer roads and stronger communities.

Who We Serve

We are built for government agencies such as VicPol, DTP, TAC, private urban planning companies, and Victorian road users seeking up‑to‑date and relevant road incident information in their local area. We aim to be a free and accessible site to ensure road safety and reduce road incidents throughout the state.

How To Utilise The Site

Our site allows users to view collated data from various sources and transform raw data into digestible insights. With filters, data can be trimmed down to display what is relevant to you and your needs without clutter or overwhelm. Navigate to the “Data” page, apply your desired filters, and quickly find the information you need with ease.
